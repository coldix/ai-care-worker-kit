# Keeping a simple weekly timesheet

*Last checked 22 Oct 2026. General information only.*

A timesheet is your memory. It's what you build invoices from, it backs you up if a provider queries your hours, and it's part of the records the ATO expects you to keep for 5 years ([ATO: record keeping](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/preparing-lodging-and-paying/record-keeping-for-business/overview-of-record-keeping-rules-for-business)).

Write it down the same day. For each visit note the date, the client code (initials or "Client A"), start and finish times, the service type, a few words about what you did, and any kilometres if the provider has agreed to pay for them. A notes app on your phone works fine, and so does the [timesheet template](../templates/timesheet.csv).

At the end of the week, paste your notes into the [timesheet prompt](../prompts/weekly-timesheet.md) to tidy them into a table, then into the [invoice prompt](../prompts/invoice-from-my-week.md) to make the invoices. Keep the timesheet in the same folder as the invoices it produced.
