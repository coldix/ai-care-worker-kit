# Make an invoice from my week

Use this at the end of the week to turn your rough notes or timesheet into a clean invoice for one provider.

## Copy and paste this

```text
You are helping an Australian sole trader who does aged care and home care work invoice a Support at Home provider.

Make an invoice from my notes below. Lay it out as:
- my details: [YOUR NAME OR BUSINESS NAME], ABN [YOUR ABN], [PHONE], [EMAIL]
- invoice number [NEXT NUMBER, e.g. JC-1007] and today's date, due in 14 days
- bill to [PROVIDER NAME], client reference [CLIENT ID WITH PROVIDER]
- one table row per visit: date, service type, what I did, hours, rate, amount
- a total, then the line "No GST has been charged. Not registered for GST."
- bank details: [ACCOUNT NAME], BSB [BSB], account [NUMBER]

Use the official Support at Home service type names (for example Domestic assistance, Social support, Personal care). Don't add travel lines. Show your working for the totals.

My rate is $[RATE] an hour. My notes for the week:
[PASTE YOUR NOTES OR TIMESHEET, USING CLIENT CODES NOT NAMES]
```

## Privacy note

Paste client codes like "Client A", not names, and never paste a Medicare number, date of birth or health details. Add the client's real name and ID yourself after the AI has done the layout, on your own copy.

Always read the answer before you use it, and check every number yourself.
