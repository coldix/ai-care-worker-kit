# Write a polite overdue reminder

Use this when an invoice is past its due date. Run it again for a second, firmer reminder.

## Copy and paste this

```text
Write a short, friendly email to a provider's accounts team about an overdue invoice. I'm an independent aged care worker and I want to keep a good relationship with them.

Invoice number: [NUMBER]
Invoice date: [DATE]
Amount: $[AMOUNT]
Due date: [DATE]
This is my [first / second / third] reminder.

For a first reminder keep it light and assume it's an oversight. For a second, ask whether anything is wrong with the invoice and who is handling it. For a third, ask for a payment date and say I'll call the accounts team. Australian English, no jargon, under 120 words, with a clear subject line.
```

## Privacy note

Leave out the client's name and health details. The invoice number is enough for the provider to find it.

Always read the answer before you use it, and check every number yourself.
