# Plan my week

Use this on Sunday to fit regular clients, one-off jobs and travel into a workable week.

## Copy and paste this

```text
Help me plan my working week as an independent aged care worker.

Regular visits:
[e.g. Client A, Mon and Thu, 2 hrs, mornings preferred; Client B, Wed, 3 hrs, any time]
One-off jobs:
[e.g. Client C, garden tidy, 4 hrs, this week]
My limits: [e.g. no work before 8am, school pickup 3pm, max 30 hrs]
Rough travel times between clients: [e.g. A to B 20 min]

Make a day-by-day plan in a table with start and finish times and travel. Point out any clashes or days that are too full, and suggest options. Then total my hours and expected income at $[RATE] an hour.
```

## Privacy note

Use client codes and suburbs at most, never street addresses.

Always read the answer before you use it, and check every number yourself.
