# Quote a new client

Use this when a family or a self-managing client asks what you'd charge for regular help.

## Copy and paste this

```text
Help me write a short, clear quote for a new aged care client. I'm an independent sole trader in [TOWN/REGION].

The work: [e.g. fortnightly clean, 2 hours, kitchen, bathroom, floors]
My rate: $[RATE] an hour [and any minimum visit, e.g. 2 hours]
When I'm available: [DAYS/TIMES]
I [am / am not] registered for GST.

Include: what's included and not included, the price per visit and per month, how I invoice (to their Support at Home provider, who must approve me first), my insurance and screening, and how to cancel a visit. Warm and plain, Australian English, under 200 words.
```

## Privacy note

Use the client's first name only, or none. Don't paste anything about their health or their budget.

Always read the answer before you use it, and check every number yourself.
