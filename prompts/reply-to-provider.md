# Reply to a provider query

Use this when a provider emails asking about an invoice, your documents or your hours, and you want a calm, clear answer.

## Copy and paste this

```text
Help me reply to this email from an aged care provider. I'm an independent worker and I want to sound professional, helpful and brief.

Their email:
[PASTE THEIR EMAIL, REMOVING CLIENT NAMES AND DETAILS]

The facts on my side:
[e.g. the visit happened on 13/10, 2 hours, approved in the care plan; my insurance renewed on 1/10 and I've attached the new certificate]

Answer each of their questions in order, say what I'm attaching, and suggest a next step. Australian English, no more than 150 words.
```

## Privacy note

Before pasting, replace client names with codes and delete any health information or Medicare numbers that appear in the provider's email.

Always read the answer before you use it, and check every number yourself.
