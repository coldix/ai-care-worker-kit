# My business details (private, never share)

Make this a Google Doc in your own Drive and keep it private. Don't share it with anyone, don't put it in a shared folder, and never add it to a public copy of this kit on GitHub.

## How to make it a Google Doc

1. In Google Drive, open your "Care business" folder and choose New, Google Docs, Blank document.
2. Name it "My business details" and copy everything below the line into it.
3. Fill in the blanks. Leave the sharing setting as it is ("Restricted"), so only you can open it.

When you make an invoice in Gemini, type @Drive and ask it to use "My business details", or attach the doc to the chat. That way you never retype your bank details into a chat. This only works with Gemini connected to your Google apps (step 13 of [the whole setup](../docs/start-here-whole-setup.md)). If you've left that off, copy the one line you need instead.
